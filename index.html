2. Hardware Pattern, 128th-Note & Sidechain Tools (Hold SHIFT + Step Pads 1–16)

In Pattern Mode, hold SHIFT and press step pads in Rows 1 & 2:

  • Row 1 (Pads 1–8 / Notes 32–39): 1: Clear Track | 2: Duplicate Pattern | 3/4: Shift Steps L/R | 5: Humanize | 6: Cycle Swing | 7/8: 2x / 0.5x BPM
  • Row 2 (Pads 9–16 / Notes 24–31): 9: Toggle Freeform (128th Auto-BPM) vs Quantized Rec | 10: Cycle Zoom (1X/2X/4X/8X) | 11/12: Nudge Step -1/+1 128th | 13: Sidechain ON/OFF | 14: Toggle Track Duck (D) | 15: Set Track as Sidechain Source (SC) | 16: Toggle Follow Playhead
  • Hold Any Step Pad + Turn Knobs: Knobs 1–6 write Parameter Locks; Hold Step + SHIFT + Knob 3 slides the note freely across the 128th-note grid!

3. Freeform First-Loop Auto-BPM & Sidechain Ducking

  • Freeform 128th Recording (Default): Press REC (Scene 2 / Note 83) to arm. Strike your first chop/drum key to begin timing freely—play your dirty hip-hop loop with natural swing, then tap REC on the downbeat to seal! Open-KO automatically calculates the exact BPM from your loop length and lays every hit onto the 128th-note micro-timing grid.
  • Sidechain Ducking Compressor: Melodic tracks (1–8, marked D) automatically duck when the Sidechain Source (Track 9 Kick, marked SC) hits. Switch to Master Knobs (DEVICE Note 71) and use Knob 7 (Release 40–500ms) & Knob 8 (Duck Depth 0–100%) to dial in heavy lo-fi / French house pump!
  • 8 Overhauled Punch-In FX (Row 5 Pads 0–7): 1/8 Stutter, 1/16 Beat Roll, 12-Bit SP-1200 Crush, Vinyl Brake, Resonant HPF Sweep, Resonant LPF Sub-Drop, French House SC Pump, Dub Space Echo.

4. How to Sample: Straight from Computer (YouTube/Spotify) or Line-In

A. Sample Directly from Computer Sound (YouTube, Spotify Web, SoundCloud):

  1. Select your destination slot (Tracks 1–8 for Melodic instruments, Tracks 9–16 for Drum Kits).
  2. Click [🖥️ TAB AUDIO] in the top bar.
  3. When the browser popup appears, select the "Chrome Tab" (e.g. YouTube or Spotify) and make sure "Also share tab audio" is CHECKED! Click Share.
  4. Hit Play on the YouTube/Spotify song. Open-KO records the pristine digital audio stream in real-time.
  5. Click [⏹ STOP TAB] when finished. Audio is peak-normalized to -0.5 dB and loaded into your slot! (Drum slots auto-slice into 16 slices!).

B. Sample from Line-In / AudioBox USB 96 / Synth / Phone:

  1. Connect your synth or phone to Input 1/2 of your USB Audio Interface (e.g. PreSonus AudioBox USB 96).
  2. In Open-KO, click [⚙ SETUP] and ensure your interface is chosen under Audio Input.
  3. Select the target track slot.
  4. Click [🎙 LINE/MIC] (or press Shift + REC on the APC Key 25 Mk1).
  5. Play your instrument or audio source.
  6. Click [⏹ STOP REC] (or press Shift + REC again). The sample is normalized and ready to play!
