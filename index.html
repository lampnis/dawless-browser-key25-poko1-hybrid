2. Hardware Pattern, 128th-Note & Sidechain Tools (Hold SHIFT + Step Pads 1–16)

  • Row 1 (Pads 1–8 / Notes 32–39): 1: Clear Track | 2: Duplicate Pattern | 3/4: Shift Steps L/R | 5: Humanize | 6: Swing | 7/8: 2x / 0.5x BPM
  • Row 2 (Pads 9–16 / Notes 24–31): 9: Freeform vs Quantized Rec | 10: Cycle Zoom 1X–8X | 11/12: Nudge -1/+1 128th | 13: Sidechain ON/OFF | 14: Track Duck (D) | 15: Set Sidechain Source (SC) | 16: Page Follow
  • Hold Any Active Step Pad: Inspect without deleting! Turn Knobs 1–6 to P-Lock, or turn SHIFT + Knob 3 to slide across the 128th-note pocket.

3. Freeform First-Loop Auto-BPM & Sidechain Ducking

  • Freeform 128th Recording (Default): Press REC to arm, strike your first chop to start timing, jam your groove freely, and tap REC on the downbeat to auto-lock BPM and place every hit on the 128th grid.
  • Sidechain Ducking Compressor: Melodic tracks (D) duck whenever the trigger source (default Track 9 SC) hits. Switch to Master Knobs (DEVICE) and dial Knob 7 (Release) and Knob 8 (Depth & Comp Vol).

4. Sampling from YouTube / Spotify Tab or Line-In

Tab Audio (YouTube / Spotify Web): Select destination track → click TAB AUDIO → choose Chrome Tab → check "Also share tab audio" → play audio → click STOP TAB.

Line-In / AudioBox USB 96: Pick your interface in SETUP → select track → press Shift + REC (or click LINE / MIC) → play → press again to normalize & auto-slice.
